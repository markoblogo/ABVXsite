Spot-Market Handbook is the English free edition of the market guide developed around the Spike Spot Index and the broader 1D3X / MN7R market-infrastructure ecosystem.

It is written for readers who need a practical map of how physical agricultural commodity markets actually work beyond futures screens, headlines and isolated broker quotes:
- brokers and traders;
- exporters, processors and commercial operators;
- analysts, founders and market researchers;
- teams building benchmark, monitoring and market-intelligence products.

The handbook focuses on the operational market layer:
- how basis, delivery terms and logistics shape real price formation;
- why liquidity and execution discipline matter more than abstract market commentary;
- how fragmented physical-market signals become benchmarks and usable reference pricing;
- how respondent-based data collection, filtering and publication turn noisy inputs into structured market infrastructure.

Inside the MN7R Commodity Brokerage Library, this edition expands the frame beyond a purely local Ukrainian reading and presents the spot-market logic in a form that is easier to use for international readers.

The publication is free. It is intended as both a practical reading entry-point and a public-facing reference for the market-infrastructure work that also powers Spike Spot Index.
