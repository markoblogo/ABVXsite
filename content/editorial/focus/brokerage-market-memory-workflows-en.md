# **Market Memory and Workflows for Brokerage Teams**

Brokerage teams accumulate valuable context in conversations: who is interested, what specification was discussed, which logistics assumptions remain open and when to follow up. When that memory stays in individual inboxes or personal files, handovers become difficult and the same questions are repeated. A shared workflow can connect a client record to the relevant market discussion and deal status.

MN7R is designed around brokerage operations and execution coordination. Its usefulness depends on disciplined data entry, clear ownership and a workflow that reflects how a team actually works. The purpose is not to automate a broker out of the loop; it is to give the team a more legible place to coordinate judgment and follow-through.
