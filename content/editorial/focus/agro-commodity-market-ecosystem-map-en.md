# **Agro Commodity Market Ecosystem Map**

A market ecosystem map is useful when it shows how a participant moves from observation to interpretation and then to action. Trading and brokerage tools organize deal work; monitoring surfaces collect signals around prices and conditions; benchmarks provide a defined reference; public fronts explain products and create a route for partners to engage.

The layers exchange context, but they should not be confused. A monitoring alert may prompt a question without establishing a price. An index may describe a defined reference without being a tradable offer. A public landing can explain a workflow without performing it. Mapping these roles gives teams a clearer vocabulary for evaluating tools and their handoffs.
