Solar proposals often use different assumptions, financing structures and scopes, which makes side-by-side comparison harder than it first appears. This book teaches homeowners how to find the important inputs and normalize them before deciding.

Readers work through cash price, financed principal, system size, equipment, production estimates, warranties, exclusions and installer-specific questions. A claim decoder and calculation layer show what common sales language may mean, what still needs verification, and where an apparently attractive offer may depend on different assumptions.

The completed decision packet preserves the comparison, open questions, written answers and final reasoning. It supports a better purchasing process without recommending a particular installer or replacing financial, legal or technical advice.
