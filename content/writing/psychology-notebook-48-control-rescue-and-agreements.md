_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The wish to control everything arises as a defence against the fear of helplessness. But the greater the strain of constant control, the less attention and energy remain for coping with immediate threats.

---

The rescuer wants things to follow their own idea of what is best, rather than simply wanting to rescue.

---

The problem with manipulation is that, over time, it costs more than making agreements.

---

It is impossible to help someone who does not want to change anything themselves.

---

Nothing helps us recognise our limits quite like being unable to help another person.
