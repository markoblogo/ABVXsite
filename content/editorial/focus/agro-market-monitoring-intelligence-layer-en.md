# **Monitoring and Intelligence for Agro Markets**

Market intelligence begins with disciplined observation. For an agricultural trader, a price movement may need to be read alongside freight availability, port conditions, weather, policy changes, crop expectations and news. A monitoring layer brings selected signals into a view that can be revisited, compared and discussed instead of relying only on memory. [Cropto Monitor](/work/cropto-monitor) and [Last30Days Cropto](/work/last30days-cropto) represent different monitoring approaches in this portfolio.

An alert is a prompt for attention, not a complete explanation or a recommendation. Good intelligence surfaces retain enough provenance for a person to inspect the source, timing and scope of a signal. The goal is to reduce the cost of gathering context while keeping uncertainty and interpretation visible.
