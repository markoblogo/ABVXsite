_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Acknowledging your resources, capabilities and limitations helps you avoid enormous expenditure in both immediate and long-term adaptation.

---

An adult is someone who copes, and who can assess and manage resources.

---

Our capacity to adapt consists of innate qualities, individual characteristics, access to material resources, social support, and a system of beliefs and values.

---

How someone treats their resources, invests in them, manages them and replenishes them largely determines their risk of maladaptation.

---

Change requires more than a decision and a choice. It also requires enough resources to sustain different choices. When resources are lacking, it is important to examine the economics: where most of them are going right now, how they are being spent, and whether anything is being invested in replenishing them.

---

Nothing is more irresponsible than taking responsibility for something you do not have the capacity to handle.
