_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The narcissism trap: wanting to be unique, but according to a ready-made, socially approved model.

---

A person's conception of their own unhappiness can be built on a life that is relatively comfortable.

---

There is nothing more boring than being interested only in yourself and demanding that the world revolve around that interest.

---

In ambivalent attachment, it is not merely ‘the farther away, the closer’, but simultaneously ‘the closer, the farther away’.

---

By suppressing girls' ability to say no, parents raise a gift for someone else: a girl who cannot refuse.

---

The magic of a narcissist is that, in them, we begin to hallucinate an improved version of ourselves.

---

Chaos and uncertainty can be a way to control a partner: the person spends all their energy trying to clarify things, leaving none for anything else.

---

Falling for someone unavailable: I love the person who treats me well in my imagination, although in reality that person treats me neither well nor badly; they simply do not engage at all.

---

Repeatedly ending up exploited can sometimes be a way for someone to feel needed and indispensable.

---

A prohibition increases the value of pleasure.
