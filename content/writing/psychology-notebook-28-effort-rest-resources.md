_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Rest is an important part of work.

---

When you look not at what someone is trying to portray but at the person behind the performance, it is easier not to get drawn into the performance itself.

---

Sometimes shock is needed to set further adaptation in motion. The important thing is not to get stuck in it.

---

Our immediate adaptation depends on how quickly we mobilise. Long-term adaptation, though, depends on how we can calm down and distribute our strength.

---

Fixation on novelty eventually disrupts adaptation, because constant impressions stop being a resource and become exhausting and destabilising.

---

Adaptation is a process of moving towards spending fewer resources on reactions in situations of challenge or threat.

---

How do you assess whether a strategy for coping with challenge, threat or loss is adaptive or maladaptive? By the possibilities for replenishing the resources spent on adaptation.

---

Sometimes building a new adaptive repertoire is difficult because it requires changing your image of yourself from someone who cannot cope to someone who can.

---

Adaptability depends on a broad range of adaptive strategies and the ability to assess realistically the resources immediately available for a particular strategy.

---

Boredom is exhausted disgust.

---

Creativity is a way of living through the unbearable.

---

A large number of obligations may be a desperate attempt to buy a sense of your own importance. The more you owe everyone, the more important you are.

---

Believing that very little effort can bring a very big result usually leads not to a result but to a very great deal of effort.

---

Love is what is stronger than all the things we get wound up about.

---

In cruelty’s shadow lies weakness. In domineering behaviour’s shadow lies powerlessness.

---

Sometimes keeping yourself exhausted is a way to cope with anxiety. When there is no strength for anything, there is not much strength for worrying either.

---

Contempt for others is a defensive response when the sense of your own worth is wounded. What I despise cannot pose a significant threat to my sense of dignity.

---

Consistency and movement in one direction produce more results than bursts in different directions, if only because a burst usually consumes more strength than the progress it brings.

---

Self-destruction is often self-punishment for guilt you have put on yourself.

---

Adaptability is not about indifference to circumstances but acceptance. Different things can happen in life; acceptance may take time, but ultimately lets you preserve your strength.
