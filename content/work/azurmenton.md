AzurMenton is a hospitality and booking interface designed for a small apartment ecosystem in Menton, France.

The project presents a focused seaside-stay surface rather than a generic rental listing. It brings together apartment visibility, direct request flow, multilingual access and local context around a small set of beachside apartments.

The system combines:
- presentation surfaces for the apartments,
- booking-oriented navigation,
- multilingual guest access,
- local Menton atmosphere,
- lightweight hospitality operations.

The public site is organized around direct guest discovery. Visitors can compare apartment options, understand the Menton location, review practical stay context and move toward a direct availability request without passing through a large marketplace interface.

As a system, AzurMenton sits in the ABVX ecosystem as a commercial presentation and interface project. It is not a protocol, AI workflow, publishing system or market infrastructure layer. Its role is narrower and more operational: a polished web surface for hospitality communication, guest orientation and lightweight booking intake.

That makes it part of Commercial Sites & Interfaces inside Systems: a small operational web system where clarity, trust, local positioning and direct communication matter more than platform complexity.
