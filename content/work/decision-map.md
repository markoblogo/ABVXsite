Decision Map is a structured protocol for AI-assisted strategic decision-making.

The system is designed for complex product, positioning and marketing scenarios where multiple variables, assumptions, risks and narratives must be mapped before action.

Instead of treating AI as a source of random ideas, Decision Map treats it as a structured reasoning layer:
- mapping assumptions,
- comparing strategic paths,
- exposing contradictions,
- clarifying dependencies,
- organizing decision pressure.

The project belongs to the broader AI-native development ecosystem around operational workflows, orchestration systems and protocolized interfaces.
