# Travel Override Template

## Scope

This override applies to travel/product guides, destination writing, and practical recommendations.

## Extends

- `WRITING`
- `VOICE`
- `READER_EFFECT`

## Replaces

PLACEHOLDER: `NONE` (default)

## Rule additions

- `locality`: placeholder fields for region-specific reliability
- `operational_detail`: placeholders for actionable details
- `verification`: placeholders for freshness and update constraints

## Governance

- Prioritize practical utility and safety over narrative.
- Keep recommendation claims clearly bounded in time and geography.
