Toki Pona Free Kits & Translations is a free publishing line for downloadable reader kits, short translations and companion materials around toki pona.

The line supports the broader ABVX toki pona publishing work by giving readers an easy entry point before they buy a book, explore a series or work with sitelen pona more deeply.

It includes reader guides, short classical translations, practice materials and companion resources connected to the Stoic Wisdom in Toki Pona and Chinese Wisdom in toki pona lines.

The purpose is practical: help readers start reading, compare formats, download material and experiment with minimalist language without friction.
