_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Love as a sense of grace, wonder and presence in the world is a force, but is not movement itself. Love is the fuel for action, rather than the action itself.

---

If what you need from a partner is complete acceptance, unconditional love, patience with every outburst, the abandonment of their own life, and the freedom to respond to all this with arbitrary emotional reactions, then that partner's name is a mother during her infant's first year of life.

---

Symbiotic relationships offer an illusion of safety, but take away opportunities for personal growth and equality within the relationship.

---

Falling in love with the unavailable: keeping enchantment untouched by disappointment.

---

Refusing to grow up means choosing a dependent position. An adult is free, not joyless.

---

Sometimes trying to have your needs met in a relationship without clarifying whether your partner can meet them, or whether another person can meet them at all, is like trying to buy a little cake in a hardware shop.

---

It is hard to truly know yourself if you try to learn about yourself only from other people's judgements.
