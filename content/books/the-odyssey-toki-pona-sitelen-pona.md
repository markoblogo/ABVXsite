The Odyssey is a story of travel, identity, temptation, endurance and return. This edition retells Homer’s epic in Toki Pona and pairs the text with Sitelen Pona, allowing readers to encounter a familiar classical work through a radically compact language.

It is designed for readers who want substantial Toki Pona reading practice, enjoy comparing how meaning survives translation, or simply want to experience the journey of Odysseus in a new linguistic form.
