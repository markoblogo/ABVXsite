_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

_Cover image supplied from the owner-authorized Psychology Notebook reserve._

Egoism and self-centredness are exhausting when you think the world is obliged to revolve around you, while inside there is a resounding nothing.

---

Devaluation leads to a chronic feeling of being deprived.

---

What keeps someone in an unbearable situation is often not so much strength as the hope that, if they endure a little longer, everything will turn out as they dream.

A reminder: there is no World Bank of Suffering where investing enough pain entitles you to anything you want.

---

Drama is like a pepper plaster for a sore back: one set of sensations distracts from another, stronger one. Instead of thinking about our real problems, it is much more entertaining to think that happiness could have been possible this way, and this way, and this way.

---

People who suffer because nobody notices them usually spend a great deal of energy on camouflage, blending into the surroundings and keeping their heads down. ‘I will sit in a corner, cover myself completely and stop breathing. Anyone who needs me will notice.’

---

Some people are sure they are unwelcome everywhere because they stand up for the truth. In fact, they are unwelcome because they are bitter and passive-aggressive under the cover of ‘Nobody likes the truth.’ Chekhov was good at characters like that: walking around, grumbling and grumbling.

---

The idea that other people are lucky and life gives them everything, while I am unlucky and life gives me nothing, is a cosy refuge. It permits endless concessions to yourself and avoidance of everyday routine. Unlucky is unlucky—why do anything?

---

Sometimes a person fights much more desperately for their unhappiness than for their happiness. Because unhappiness is a good lever of influence.

---

Giving up the idea of being unhappy can be frightening, because suffering seems to create a plot and make life interesting.

If you have not suffered, it is as though you have not lived.

---

Drama is enjoyment of impossibility.
