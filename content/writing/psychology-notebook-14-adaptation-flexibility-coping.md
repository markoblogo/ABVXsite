_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Adaptation is a balance between capabilities and needs.

---

— You cannot warm up the frost with nothing but your naked self.

---

Sometimes the fight response turns against yourself.

---

Being adaptable does not mean putting up with things. If we are merely enduring them, adaptation is not happening.

---

Constant rushing is one expression of the flight response.

---

Our capacity to adapt is a dynamic system, not a rigid structure.

---

Tell me how you adapt, and I will tell you who you are.

---

In the long run, resilience without flexibility leads to maladaptation.

---

Helplessness is for an imagined caretaker.

Not coping for somebody's benefit.

---

An excessive fixation on experiences eventually turns into exhaustion.

---

Adaptation means turning the unfamiliar and threatening into something familiar that you can cope with.

---

It is not fate that makes someone a loser, but choosing quick pleasure over consistent action.

---

What helps us adapt in the short term often reduces our adaptability in the long term.

---

A pronounced character helps us adapt in the first half of life and gets in the way in the second.

---

Discipline means managing and respecting our main non-renewable resource: time.

---

Adaptation is when encountering a stressor no longer demands a substantial expenditure of resources.

---

The wish for total control as a sign of distrust in your own ability to handle surprises.

---

Tolerance of uncertainty rests on faith in your own strength and ability to cope.

---

Sometimes, to cope, it is important to accept your right not to cope all the time.

---

Chronic stress is often the result of pressing harder on the accelerator instead of using the brake.
