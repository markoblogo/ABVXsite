_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The defense mechanism of projection: an external enemy is needed to make the hostility within bearable at all.

---

When the urge to rescue arises, it is worth asking: what am I trying to save in myself through all this commotion?

---

When avoidance becomes a person's main survival strategy, they often start simply overlooking and ignoring whatever causes inner discomfort.

---

At some point in addiction, what becomes visible is no longer the person, but the thing on which they depend.

---

We gradually become what we depend on.

---

The dependent person's pleasure is being unable to endure. The codependent person's pleasure is endless endurance.

---

For a person with an addiction, the craving is like a broad highway leading towards use, while the route to healthy alternatives may be only a half-overgrown path.

---

Every maladaptive strategy was, at some point, more than adaptive. But the situation in which it was adaptive has passed, while the strategy remains and consumes an enormous amount of resources.

---

The motivation to avoid bad things as much as possible creates a very different life story from the pursuit of good things.

---

When nothing feels worthwhile, everything can be postponed.
