_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The collapse of illusions clears space for life.

---

An inability to grieve leads to a build-up of unnecessary things in life.

---

Giving up omnipotence and recognising the limits of your abilities involves sadness, but brings freedom.

---

There are two ways of working with resentment. The first is to try to use resentment to make the world better for yourself. The second is to mourn your losses and soften your demands, or work out where those demands are realistic and where no amount of resentment will achieve anything.

---

To grieve is to confront and grow accustomed to the reality of loss. To avoid grieving is to keep the dead among the living.
