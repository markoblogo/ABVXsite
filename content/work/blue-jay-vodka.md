Blue Jay Vodka is a brand landing page presenting the vodka through a cold alpine visual world, product storytelling and the “Intelligent by Nature” identity.

The landing combines:
- brand positioning,
- product and packaging presentation,
- craft and ingredient storytelling,
- an interactive visual experience,
- direct contact navigation.

The project translates the Blue Jay identity into a focused digital presentation surface for the brand.
