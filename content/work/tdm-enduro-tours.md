TDM Enduro Tours is a commercial website redesign for enduro, pitbike and ATV rides based in Pirohiv, Kyiv. The project refreshes the logo, visual identity, Ukrainian copy and image direction, bringing the ride experience and base facilities into one clear public presentation.

The redesigned site combines:
- ride formats and pricing with experience-based selection,
- an introduction to the base, equipment and preparation process,
- refreshed visual assets, concept imagery and original ride videos,
- practical FAQs, weather context and contact paths,
- TDM Pixel Ride, a playable enduro mini-game embedded in the page.

The site is currently presented as a design prototype on the temporary domain [tdm.abvx.xyz](https://tdm.abvx.xyz/). Its generative images illustrate the visual concept; ride availability and booking conditions are confirmed by the TDM team.

Within Systems, TDM belongs to Commercial Sites & Interfaces: a website where branding, copy, visual storytelling and interactive elements help visitors understand an outdoor service and find the next step.
