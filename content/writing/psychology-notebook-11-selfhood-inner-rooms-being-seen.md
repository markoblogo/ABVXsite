_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The exercise of will rests largely on self-definition.

---

The subject emerges from the shock of encountering the Other.

---

Growing up means gradually creating a stable vantage point from which to observe yourself.

---

When character and talent are in conflict, talent usually loses.

---

A sense of self is not an intellectual concept. It is a warm presence.

---

A role model is always an inner rival. To become more original than the person you are copying.

---

It is always interesting to imagine someone's inner space. A mansion for one person, a tiny room full of junk for another, an unfinished building for someone else, a neat little house for another.

---

Psycho-acrobatics with self-directed aggression: stabbing yourself in the back.

---

Without a concept of yourself, it is hard to build a concept for yourself.

---

Tell me what your hell is like, and I will tell you who you are.

---

The idea that it is 'too late to start' disguises the fact that starting might be difficult.

---

People sometimes put so much effort into avoiding an encounter with themselves.

---

— How do you stay out of sight and make sure everybody notices you?

---

Tolerance is a sign of confidence. The less confidence there is, the more dangerous diversity seems.

---

The trouble with the flight response is that you always take yourself with you.

---

Intolerance of the variety of ways people can be is such a clear sign of insecurity.

---

No amount of 'becoming like somebody else' can compare with 'being who you are'.

---

Being the injured party as a way of being noticed, if only by yourself.

---

How am I supposed to fight my inner demons when I am a devil myself?

---

People often want confirmation of their idea of themselves and the world more than they want change.
