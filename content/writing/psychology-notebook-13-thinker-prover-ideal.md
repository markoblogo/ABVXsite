_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Greed always goes hand in hand with boredom.

---

An enemy is a reservoir into which you can drain your own shadow side.

---

The simpler a person's consciousness, the less diversity it can withstand.

---

A row is a way of getting closer to the truth and clarifying how things really stand.

---

Romanticising means trying to cover the gaps, inconsistencies and failures of the real with a veil of the imaginary.

---

Our thinking consists of a thinker and a prover. Whatever the thinker thinks, the prover will find arguments for it.

---

Looking for an external enemy as a way of avoiding your own inner conflict. Projective mechanisms.

---

Love bombing and ignoring, as expressions of idealisation and de-idealisation, have the same target: to fill the other person, who is under attack, with your presence.

---

Perfectionism often leads to inaction rather than action: if it cannot be perfect straight away, there is no point even starting.

---

Half of what we think today is yesterday's thoughts. That is why our attitudes are so hard to change.

---

Behind the desire for the ideal is fear of the real. While I am fantasising about the ideal, I am protected from the desert of the real.

---

Thoughts are the matrix through which we construct our reality. Paying attention to your inner space is an essential part of mental hygiene.

---

It is good when someone does not want to discuss the rules, because it means that, with this person, there are no rules to the game.

---

Shame is the pain the real feels when the ideal attacks it.

---

On projection. The wary thought 'Nobody treats me right' disguises the way a person treats everybody else wrong.

---

Resentment will always find a gap between expectation and reality in which to flourish.

---

Some people are insinuating mirrors. In their company, you think they understand you so well, when they are actually mirroring you.

---

Judging your own strength can be difficult, because it means giving up the idea of being grand and all-powerful.

---

A wound is like a prism: you do not see the world itself, but assemble everything through the facets of the wound.

---

Strategy without tactics means lying in the direction of what you want and believing in the power of thought. Tactics without strategy means acting chaotically, in short dashes.
