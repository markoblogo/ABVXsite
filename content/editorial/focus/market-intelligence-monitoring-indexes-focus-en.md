# **Market Intelligence, Monitoring and Indexes in Focus**

Market intelligence tools help participants gather observations and revisit them with more context. This group spans commodity monitoring, recent market mentions, spot references and index views. Together, [Cropto Monitor](/work/cropto-monitor), [Last30Days Cropto](/work/last30days-cropto), [SPIKE](/work/spike-spot-commodity-index-ukraine), [UGA Index](/work/uga-index) and related surfaces address different questions: what may be changing, where a reference comes from and how a market signal can be inspected over time.

The distinctions are essential. News aggregation is not a benchmark; a benchmark is not a forecast; an alert does not confirm a trade. This catalogue groups the tools because users may consult them during one research process, while preserving each product’s separate purpose and limitations.
