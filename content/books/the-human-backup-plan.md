If the person who understands the household, business or digital systems suddenly becomes unavailable, the immediate problem is operational: what must continue, where the essential information lives, and who can act safely?

The Human Backup Plan helps map money, accounts, devices, documents, digital assets, recovery paths and business responsibilities. Its 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day structure turns a broad continuity problem into a usable handoff plan.

This is neither a password book nor a will. It is a practical framework for deciding what trusted people need to know, what should be kept secure, and what should eventually be kept, transferred, cancelled, archived or deleted.
