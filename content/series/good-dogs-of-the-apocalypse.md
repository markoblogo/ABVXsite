## **Good Dogs of the Apocalypse: A Darkly Comic LitRPG Series**

What happens when the rules of the world change—and the dogs understand those rules better than the people around them?

*Good Dogs of the Apocalypse* is a darkly comic, dog-centered LitRPG series set in a post-apocalyptic city reshaped by the System. At its heart is Mriya, a former shelter dog whose new abilities bring choices as often as they bring power. The people in her life may not see the System, but they live with the consequences of its bargains, classifications, and rewards.

This is progression fantasy with muddy paws on the ground. Survival means finding shelter, keeping water moving, making a route safe, and deciding what people owe one another. A new skill can help—but every useful arrangement has a cost, and no rule can make trust automatic.

## **Three books, one changing world**

The series follows Mriya and her community as their world grows from one fragile home into a much wider network of neighbors, trades, and competing institutions. Each novel builds on the relationships and choices that came before, so the best way to read the series is in order.

1. ***Good Dog, Bad System*** — The first book introduces Mriya to a world where dogs can see the System and its new possibilities. As she gathers people and dogs around a place they can call home, Mriya must decide what kind of community she is willing to build—and what she will refuse to become for its sake.  
2. ***Good Dog, Bad District*** — The Pack Hearth grows into a district with practical trades, classes, and rival institutions. Cooperation can keep resources moving, but it can also turn one person into the answer everyone waits for. Mriya and her neighbors have to find ways to share skills and responsibility without giving up their independence.  
3. ***Good Dog, Bad Authority*** — As routes and rival forms of protection shape the district, more people look to Mriya to settle what happens next. But stewardship is not the same as control. The third novel asks whether a community can take responsibility together without making one person the authority over everyone else.

## **A canine apocalypse about more than leveling up**

Readers looking for a LitRPG with a canine heroine will find System progression, new abilities, and the familiar pressure of choices with consequences. They will also find a story interested in what happens between the dramatic moments: the work that keeps a shelter running, the favors that become obligations, and the small decisions that determine whether a place feels like home.

The series balances dark humor with the serious business of survival. Its System may have labels for everything, but Mriya’s world resists easy categories. A person can want protection and still value independence. A promise can matter without becoming a command. And sometimes the most important power is the ability to say no—and still be welcome afterward.

Start with *Good Dog, Bad System* and follow Mriya’s story through *Good Dog, Bad District* and *Good Dog, Bad Authority*: a darkly comic progression fantasy about dogs, people, and the difficult work of building a future together.
