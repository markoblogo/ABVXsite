_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Looking for someone to blame is a good distraction from accepting your own responsibility.

---

Separation means not offloading your choices and decisions onto someone else.

---

Self-accusation is often the opposite of a genuine apology.

---

Interestingly, maintaining a child's position without responsibility or resources ultimately takes more effort than adult life, with its own responsibility and resources.

---

Frustration at a mistake is a natural part of learning. But the mistake itself is also part of learning. If frustration over a mistake becomes too strong, it blocks learning, reducing adaptability in the long term.
