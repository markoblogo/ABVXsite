The world has ended, everyone is behaving irrationally, and Marcus Aurelius still wants you to focus on what you can control.

This comic thought experiment moves practical Stoic themes into a zombie apocalypse: panic, irritating people, bad judgments, limited resources and the stubborn need to behave decently when circumstances become absurd. It is philosophy through satire rather than a historical edition of Marcus Aurelius.
