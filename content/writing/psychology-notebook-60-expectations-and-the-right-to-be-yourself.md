_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Discontent is a way to bargain for more privileges.

---

Realistic expectations of yourself and others are a foundation of psychological well-being.

---

A blocked impulse to attack presents a difficult choice: either overcome fear and defend yourself, or endure it and feel the wound to your self-respect.

---

It is not enough simply to recognize and understand the reality of yourself; it is also important to bear it: real, not ideal.

---

Psychotherapy is an investigation that, in the course of things, leads back to yourself.

---

An unpleasant fact: if life has dealt you a blow, there is no mechanism guaranteeing you compensation for having suffered.

---

Deliberate overload is a peculiar form of regulation: when you have no strength left, you also have no strength to attack yourself.

---

Being constantly dissatisfied is one way of feeling influential.

---

A person with impaired autonomy swings between “Tell me what to do” and “Don't tell me what to do”.

---

Immaturity shows itself in the bright idea that everyone else is to blame, while the immature person is just a sweet, wounded little kitty.
