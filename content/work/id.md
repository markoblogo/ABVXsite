ID is a protocol-oriented project inside the ABVX AI-native ecosystem.

The system explores structured identity, references and coordination logic across workflows, systems and operational environments.

Rather than acting as a conventional application, ID functions as a protocol layer: a way of organizing references, relationships and operational consistency between systems.
