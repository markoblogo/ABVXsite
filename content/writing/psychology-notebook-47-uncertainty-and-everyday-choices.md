_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The stronger our inner sense of danger, the more the mind reaches for a simple, unambiguous picture of the world.

---

For an anxious person, freedom is experienced as uncertainty.

---

Indecision and treading water often consume more energy than taking a decisive step.

---

Pessimism is a peculiar way of protecting yourself from failure. If I am already upset in advance, nothing can upset me.

---

Choice is the ability to assess not what would be best to acquire, but what would be easier to give up.

---

Major changes in life, for better or worse, are usually preceded by many small everyday choices.

Of course there is also chance: catastrophe, trouble that kicks the door in. Chance does not always bring loss; something good can turn up too. Yet a person returns from both grief and joy to their habitual sense of themselves, and without changing their choices, carries on living as the person they were.
