_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Confidence is not built on preventing every mistake, because that is impossible. Confidence is the ability to deal with the consequences of mistakes promptly, rather than endlessly thinking through every scenario in advance.

---

When it is hard to tolerate the frustration of something not working out, it is always too late, and any undertaking is definitely not for me.

---

Authorship requires the ability to take a substantial risk so that something can be expressed and heard.

---

It is safe to be a failure: nobody expects anything, and nobody asks questions. If you succeed, and succeed well, people might expect something from you.

---

Competence is the ability to see the simplest solution in a chaos of possibilities.

---

The ability to learn well has a reverse side: bad things are easy to learn too.

---

If you were understood instantly, effortlessly and completely, you were not understood: you are in front of a mirror.

---

Choice is an assessment of losses. Ruminating over a choice can be an attempt to avoid facing those losses.

---

I didn't get what I wanted, but I got what I needed.

---

Some people will not so easily show that anything in life pleases them. Their motto: you can't disappoint someone who's already disappointed!
