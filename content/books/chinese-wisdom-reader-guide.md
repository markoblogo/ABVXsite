Free Kit / Reader’s Guide: Chinese Wisdom in toki pona is a companion guide for readers entering the Chinese Wisdom in toki pona publishing line.

The guide explains how to approach the editions as layered reading objects rather than simple one-to-one translations. The line brings together classical Chinese source texts, English orientation, toki pona renderings and sitelen pona-ready reading surfaces.

Its role is practical: help readers understand the structure of the books, move between languages, recognize repeated concepts and use toki pona as a slow-reading method for classical Chinese thought.

The guide is intended for:

- readers beginning with the Chinese Wisdom in toki pona line,
- toki pona learners,
- sitelen pona readers,
- and anyone comparing minimalist language with classical philosophical texts.

As part of the Toki Pona Free Kits & Translations line, the guide acts as a low-friction entry point before moving into the full books, companion sites and paid editions.
