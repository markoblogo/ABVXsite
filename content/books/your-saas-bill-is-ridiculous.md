Your SaaS Bill Is Ridiculous is a short discovery booklet about open-source tools that are genuinely worth knowing about, and about the operational bill that usually comes back the moment somebody says “we can just self-host it.”

It is not a catalogue and not a manifesto. It is a compact editorial field guide: blunt verdicts, one-page project reads, a few useful rabbit holes, and a closing reminder that software costs rarely disappear. They move into hosting, maintenance, GPUs, APIs, migrations and human attention.

The booklet is built for people who are tempted by expensive SaaS fatigue but do not want fake savings stories. Some tools here clearly earn a place in a real stack. Some deserve a cautious trial first. Some are better treated as a warning that paying for the hosted product may still be the sane decision.
