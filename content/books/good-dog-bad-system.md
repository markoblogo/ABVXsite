## **Good Dog, Bad System: A Darkly Comic System Apocalypse LitRPG**

What if the first hero of the apocalypse wasn’t a warrior, a chosen one, or even a person—but an older shelter dog with a sore hip and a sharp nose for trouble?

*Good Dog, Bad System* is a darkly comic post-apocalyptic LitRPG about Mriya, a shelter dog who finds a home with Nika just as the world begins to change. When a mysterious System offers dogs new abilities and a choice of roles, Mriya discovers that surviving the apocalypse may be easier than deciding what kind of power is safe to accept.

## **A System apocalypse seen through a dog’s eyes**

Mriya understands the world through scent, movement, pain, food, and the people who return. That perspective makes the collapse feel immediate and grounded: a strange smell in a tunnel, an uneasy trip through the city, a damaged home, and the question of who can be trusted when familiar rules stop working.

The System introduces the recognizable pleasures of LitRPG—classes, abilities, choices, and consequences—but Mriya is not eager to follow instructions just because they appear in a prompt. The offered paths promise power, yet each comes with a different kind of influence over others. Refusing the obvious options has a cost, but accepting them without question could cost something more important.

## **An apocalypse story about loyalty and choice**

At its heart, *Good Dog, Bad System* is about the difference between care and control. Mriya wants to protect Nika and the dogs and people who gather around them. But protection can become a leash when one person—or one System—decides what everyone else needs.

As the group tries to build a refuge, they face practical problems that matter as much as any battle: water, medicine, food, safe routes, and the difficult work of sharing decisions. Their community takes shape through small acts of trust and cooperation, not through a simple promise that everything will be fine.

The book’s dark humor grows naturally from the gap between epic System language and Mriya’s grounded priorities. A grand announcement may be less urgent than a bad smell, an empty bowl, or a human making an obviously poor decision near a door.

## **A character-led LitRPG for readers who want more than leveling**

Readers looking for a post-apocalyptic LitRPG with a distinctive protagonist, meaningful choices, and a strong found-family core will find a different kind of survival story here. Mriya is not a human protagonist in a dog’s body: her instincts, loyalties, limits, and judgments shape the story on their own terms.

*Good Dog, Bad System* begins the *Good Dogs of the Apocalypse* series with a question that reaches beyond the first crisis: can a community become stronger without making anyone its property? The answer is built one choice at a time—through care, disagreement, boundaries, and the freedom to leave.

If you enjoy system apocalypse fiction, darkly comic fantasy, found-family stories, and LitRPG adventures where power has an ethical cost, meet Mriya in *Good Dog, Bad System*—the first book in *Good Dogs of the Apocalypse*.
