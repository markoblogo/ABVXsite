SPIKE SPOT COMMODITY INDEX UKRAINE is benchmark infrastructure for Ukrainian agro-commodity spot markets.

The project provides structured reference pricing around selected grain and oilseed positions, with public spot values for export and processing bases such as CPT Odesa and CPT parity Odesa.

The market problem is fragmentation. Ukrainian commodity pricing moves through exporter demand, processor demand, port access, freight, currency, product quality, logistics pressure and bilateral market indications. Without a shared reference layer, traders and analysts often work from scattered quotations rather than a comparable benchmark.

SPIKE turns respondent-partner assessments into a public market-reference layer:
- aggregating daily spot-market context,
- applying repeatable benchmark logic,
- validating submissions around a central market level,
- filtering outliers,
- publishing locked values only when respondent coverage is sufficient.

The system is not a trading platform. It does not replace brokerage, negotiation or execution. Its role is to make spot-market conditions more legible so traders, brokers, exporters, processors, analysts and other market participants can compare Ukrainian reference levels through a more stable public lens.

Within the broader Cropto and agro-market infrastructure ecosystem, SPIKE belongs to the intelligence and index layer. It sits next to monitoring terminals, benchmark projects and market-data surfaces that support coordination around physical commodity markets.

That makes it part of Monitoring & Intelligence: not execution infrastructure itself, but a benchmark layer for reference pricing, market visibility and structured Ukrainian spot-market memory.
