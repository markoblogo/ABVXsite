# **Monitoring Logistics and Market Context for Agro Trade**

A market view based on price alone can miss the conditions that shape physical movement. Freight availability, port status, weather, border procedures, policy announcements and crop developments may change the questions a team needs to ask. Monitoring tools help collect these signals into a shared picture and make it easier to return to a source when a situation changes.

Cropto Monitor focuses on commodity signals and market context; Last30Days Cropto aggregates recent mentions and summaries. They are complementary ways to scan information, not substitutes for operational confirmation. Teams still need to verify time-sensitive conditions with the relevant source before relying on them for a shipment or commercial commitment.
