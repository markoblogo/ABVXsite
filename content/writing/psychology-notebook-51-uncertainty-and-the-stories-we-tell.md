_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Uncertainty is a no that has not been said to you.

---

Waiting is so difficult because attention becomes fixed on the point when an event will happen in the future.

---

Adaptation depends not on whether all the mysteries have been solved, but on how much a person can tolerate the fact that the world is itself a mystery.

---

For those so worked up by uncertainty that they can no longer adapt, I offer this comfort: death is inevitable and certain. What more certainty do you need?

---

Uncertainty is the part of reality that has not yet been covered by a layer of meaning.

---

When you physically lose your footing, the harder you try to grab hold of something, the more you lose your balance. Adaptation works much the same way.

---

Sometimes adaptation is not an endless fight, but the ability to surrender.

---

Paying attention to the bad is important for survival, but not so good for living.

---

Meaning is a special kind of pleasure, but if that pleasure is missing, you can get by without it for a while.

---

What doesn't kill us makes us completely different.
