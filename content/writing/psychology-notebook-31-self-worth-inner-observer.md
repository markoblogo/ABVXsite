_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Therapy is a change in your concept of yourself.

---

Indifference towards yourself is a form of self-directed aggression.

---

Therapy helps you stop looking for a hero outside yourself.

---

A dependent person feels sorry for themselves; a codependent person feels sorry for the dependent person instead of themselves.

---

Through a relationship with a dependent person, the codependent person tries to buy themselves omnipotence.

---

The way a person pays for therapy reflects the way they value themselves.

---

A dependent person often arranges a failure as a reason to relapse.

---

The victim position goes like this: having offered yourself up, offer someone else up too.

---

The economy of victimhood is the wish to buy special treatment with your wounds.

---

Overcoming dependency can greatly revive your sense of dignity. Freedom is an instinct and a supreme value.

---

With impostor syndrome, it is hard to own your achievements because the person doing the owning has such a weak sense of themselves.

---

Feeling unwell is sometimes the only way the psyche can draw your attention to yourself.

---

Dependence on an outside observer’s judgement reveals the weakness of the inner observer: the point of self is barely defined.

---

Unhappiness is often the result of demands that do not fit reality. Neurosis is a kind of Tale of the Fisherman and the Fish.

---

The victim position means idealising yourself and demonising the other person. Demonising the other is itself a way of idealising yourself.

---

The victim position often obscures cause and effect. It all just happened, all by itself.

---

When people say they did something for someone else, they did it for themselves: to get what they wanted from that person.

---

A lack of moderation means poor contact with the feeling of enough. Excess does harm. “Why does a person need so much? To harm themselves.”

---

Managing your time and material resources depends directly on how much respect you have for yourself.

---

Treating yourself well is not the same as indulging yourself. Treating yourself well includes knowing how to limit what is excessive and harmful.

---
