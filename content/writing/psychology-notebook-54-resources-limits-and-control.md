_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Knowing your limits does not make you weak. On the contrary, it gives you strength.

---

Adaptation depends not on your ability to endure a shitstorm, but on your ability to get out of it promptly. The point is not to cope with the shitstorm, but with its sources.

---

Adaptability is determined not only by how a person copes, but also by how they relate to forces far greater than their individual will.

---

Willpower works where resources are available. When there is no strength left, trying to switch on your will only deepens the paralysis.

---

Hypercontrol usually does not reduce risks; it increases them by creating tension and strain and eventually reducing concentration.

---

Hypercontrol is an outward expression of chronic fear.

---

To adapt is to fit the environment without merging with it.

---

Adaptability is the ability to let the unimportant slide without letting the important slide. Though of course the temptation to do the opposite is strong.

---

Refusing to rest is, on the one hand, self-punishment and, on the other, an unrealistic idea of one's strength and how it is restored.

---

Self-sabotage is a peculiar attempt to gain control: if success is unpredictable, I can certainly arrange my own defeat! Sabotage as the illusion of authorship over a bad outcome.
