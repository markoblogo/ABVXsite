_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Bitterness is always a sign of hurt and inner distress.

---

Boredom is sometimes a way of anaesthetising unbearable pain.

---

Resentment is hatred that has run out of strength.

---

And about guilt: guilt is psychological masturbation.

---

Understanding can be an elegant way of blocking and avoiding feeling.

---

Guilt is a painkiller for those who do not know how to grieve.

---

Discontent is like whining that has acquired some confidence.

---

When somebody is bored with life, even pain counts as an event.

---

The opposite of shame is pride. The opposite of guilt is freedom.

---

Dramatising is a way of not feeling what is really troubling you.

---

Suffering often leaves no room for compassion. Pain makes a person self-centred.

---

Offering comfort does not mean stopping someone's feelings. It means knowing how to acknowledge them and be present.

---

Running away from small frustrations means running towards a big one.

---

Greed brings the boredom of never having enough rather than the joy of possession.

---

It is better to face bottomless sadness than to live through endless pain.

---

People often find suffering easier to bear than admitting there is nothing going on inside.

---

It is hard to be happy for other people when you do not know how to be happy for yourself.

---

Separation means recognising and accepting another person's emotions without servicing them.

---

Where a person overcompensates, there is pain.

---

Drama is built on finding impossibility more captivating than possibility.
