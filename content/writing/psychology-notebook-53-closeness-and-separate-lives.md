_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Closeness is an important part of life. Paradoxically, genuine closeness is possible when you can be interesting company to yourself while alone.

---

A partner can reveal a great deal about a person's relationship with themselves.

---

The foundation of separation is the ability to withstand the displeasure and hurt feelings of those close to you.

---

Overprotection is an act of violence against the formation of a personality: there is simply no room left for it to emerge and develop.

---

Nobody wants to be good for no reason. All that self-sacrifice and self-denial is an attempt to buy something so precious and important that giving oneself up seems worth the price.

---

If you love someone, your warm feelings do not make the person you love indebted to you.
— But I love you, so do everything I want.
That is not love; it is a consumer's attitude.

---

If you do something for another person, you deprive them of the chance to learn and then be able to do it themselves.

---

Autonomy rests on the ability to bear another person as another: separate and ultimately unknowable.

---

Separation is a choice between living in a prison and learning to cope with guilt.

---

It is hard to live without a sense that you have a right to yourself.
