# **Connecting Market Fronts to Underlying Infrastructure**

A commercial front is stronger when it points to the operating capability behind its promise. A page can explain whether a project is a brokerage system, a market-data view, a partner concept or a library, and show how an interested organization can continue the conversation. This gives product narrative a useful connection to real workflows and evidence.

The Focus catalogue links market-facing concepts such as [Cropto](/work/cropto), [Liqua](/work/liqua), [Trade Solution EU](/work/trade-solution-eu) and [AMI Books](/ami) to their own descriptions. Their roles differ, so clear boundaries matter: a concept page should not imply an integration, offer or live service that has not been established. A good landing helps the right reader find the right next question.
