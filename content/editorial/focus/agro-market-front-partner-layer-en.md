# **Market Fronts and Partner Landings**

A market front is the public surface through which a participant first understands a product, service or collaboration. For commodity infrastructure, a strong landing page explains the user, the problem, the operating boundary and the appropriate next step. It should connect a commercial narrative back to the actual data, workflow or partner capability behind it.

A polished page alone does not establish that a service is available, that a transaction can be executed or that a result is guaranteed. Clear fronts help interested people ask better questions and route those questions to the right team. Within this portfolio, project pages, partner concepts and the [AMI Books](/ami) library serve different roles in that public-facing layer.
