_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Neurosis is sacrifice for the sake of sacrifice.

---

Playing poor means buying attention by portraying yourself as a victim.

---

Victimhood is the sexualisation of our times.

---

The victim wants revenge, not freedom.

---

Therapy is the dismantling of the myth of your own unhappiness.

---

Victimhood is usually accompanied by a demand that the greatness of the victim be recognised.

---

To blame someone else endlessly is to remain a victim yourself.

---

The victim position rests on denying the possibilities of your own will.

---

Pity as a substitute for missing admiration. If you will not praise me, at least feel sorry for me.

---

What do people most often encounter when suffering switches off? Boredom.

---

The central idea sustaining victimhood: everyone must make allowances for my situation.

---

If you replace the word “victim” with “prey”, the status loses its charm.

---

Dependency is a way of fleeing what does not fit or satisfy us, outside or inside. Yet it is a kind of running in which the harder you run, the more firmly you remain in the unsuitable situation.

---

If mourning is withdrawing investment from the object, suffering is investing in it in order to obtain what you want.

---

Psychotherapy means replacing suffering with compassion, beginning with compassion towards yourself.

---

What instead of suffering? Making and creating are considerably more interesting than futile thrashing about.

---

Feeling like a victim is a way of feeling right. Whoever has suffered must be right.

---

A special kind of victim: the theoretically injured. “Nobody oppressed me, but they could have.”

---

Suffering, like any dependency, is absorbing until you notice how endlessly repetitive it is.

---

Escaping into drama is one way of distracting yourself from what is truly unbearable.

---
