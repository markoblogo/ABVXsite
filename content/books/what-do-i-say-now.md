Difficult workplace conversations are often hardest in the first few seconds: the issue is clear, but the words are not. This reference provides adaptable scripts for situations involving feedback, mistakes, deadlines, meetings, money, boundaries, conflict and bad news.

The scripts are starting points rather than magic phrases. Readers can choose language that fits the situation, adjust the tone, and move from avoidance or improvisation toward a clearer conversation.
