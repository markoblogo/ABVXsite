_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

If, when creating something, you take your bearings only from other people's opinions rather than your own interest and fire, you will inevitably create something average. Authorship is an expression of your own position as a subject, rather than an attempt to please an audience, especially an imaginary one.

---

Backing down and giving up in the face of significant tasks is omnipotence for the lazy. I could have, but chose not to.

---

Avoiding action in reality is a way of remaining potentially perfect.

---

The idea of perfection: to freeze in a dead world of flawlessness in the hope of receiving something alive.

---

Fantasy can be more seductive than reality because it has no sequence or gradual progress: you get everything at once. Well, you get it in fantasy. In reality, of course, you do not.
