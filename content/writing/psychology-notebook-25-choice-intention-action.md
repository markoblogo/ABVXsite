_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Assignments after a session can sometimes be apt. But wanting an assignment is often a wish to relieve anxiety rather than encounter it and find out where it comes from.

---

Hidden hostility means having a great deal of anger but being afraid to act openly. It often takes the form of useful advice and instructions on how to live properly.

---

The problem is that power does not resolve an inner conflict; it only intensifies it.

---

Suffering is camouflage for desire.

---

People who constantly want new experiences often lack not the experiences themselves but receptiveness to a moderate range of feelings. They tend to replace a lack of inner movement with external bustle.

---

A tendency to inform on people reveals a belief in an ideal parent who knows better and will decide for you.

---

The imaginary external observer who somehow looks at you the wrong way is actually an internal observer projected outwards by imagination.

---

Of course it would be nice if the world changed and responded to all our demands, taking our wishes into account. But the world usually says: I am not your mother!

---

Repeating the same destructive pattern does not leave you standing still; it leads to progressively worsening dynamics.

---

Replacing life plans and consistent action towards them with momentary passions inevitably leads to a loss of resources and ruin in every area.

---

A manipulator seems cunning until you see how pitiful and deprived of living interaction the manipulator is.

---

Taking no consistent action towards change and waiting for everything to sort itself out is a path that toughens you and teaches you to endure the unbearable. Just put up with it a little longer, and then it will definitely all happen by itself.

---

Touchiness is a currency accepted by people who cannot cope with guilt. With everyone else, it converts not into action towards the offended person but into a healthy wish to keep their distance.

---

Accepting the diversity of the world is a good sign of inner calm. The stronger the sense of threat, the more you want a simple world.

---

Resentment gives you a sense of being right.

---

It is astonishing how fiercely, decisively and courageously people sometimes defend the image of the poor little thing.

---

Suffering is an activity you can always arrange. ‘I want everything to be the way I want!’ ‘You should have done it earlier; I do not want it any more!’

---

To support adaptation, having inner and outer resources is not enough. What matters is how quickly you can access them and whether you can manage them.

---

Pseudo-adaptation can mean that, when faced with a problem, someone does not look for a solution but downplays the problem’s importance or denies its existence altogether.

---

Achieving a goal requires the ability to act consistently and not trade yourself away for little goodies instead of getting the big goodie.
