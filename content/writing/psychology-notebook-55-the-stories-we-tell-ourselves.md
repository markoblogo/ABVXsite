_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The psyche guards self-deception most fiercely.

---

Tell me what your inner space is like, and I will tell you who you are.

Mine is a great pillared hall, with niches, corridors and alcoves beyond it.

---

An enemy is a container for what you cannot bear within yourself.

---

The victim position means not seeing your own choices in your life.

---

In reality, the ego is like a circus clown who takes responsibility for everything so the audience believes everything happening is the clown's doing.

---

Most often, the person standing in your way is yourself.

---

Guilt gives the illusion of control: better that I am to blame than that the world is chaotic and exists on its own terms.

---

The thought ‘Why are they treating me like this?’ distracts from ‘Why am I treating myself like this?’

---

Fighting yourself has a serious downside: whoever wins, you will still be the loser.

---

Before changing yourself, it might be worth getting to know yourself. You might change your mind about changing.
