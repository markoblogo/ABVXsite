_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

We want to rescue those we have no faith in.

---

Envy combines wanting to possess with not wanting to act.

---

Giving up a desire as a way of avoiding the frustration of refusal.

---

Drama disguises the real problem and the unmet need.

---

Wanting to take over somebody else's story gets in the way of noticing and discovering your own.

---

Falling for troubled people means going against nature in the pursuit of drama.

---

Sometimes the determination and staying power behind the wish to remain in an unbearable situation are astonishing.

---

Suffering is a way of distracting yourself from the real problem. A pepper plaster over the actual need.

---

The desire to win, whine, buy or force love out of someone who is indifferent to you, at any cost, often underlies deeply destructive stories.

---

Unrequited love is a kind of complaint against its object: we poor unfortunates know better who that person ought to choose.

---

An excellent way of not getting what you want is not saying what you need.

---

Wanting a partner who will solve every problem for you is an unmet need for an ideal parent.

---

Giving up your desires creates permanent discontent. If you do not know what you want, how can you know what to be happy about?

---

The desire for constant euphoria conceals the desire to distract yourself from pain. Constant euphoria is practically a kind of pain in itself.

---

When choosing, it is important to understand not only what will be better, but also what will be easier to let go of.

---

An important sign of mental well-being is being able to sit quietly with yourself without wanting to occupy, distract or entertain yourself.

---

The role of the family's disaster, fool or poor unfortunate falls to whoever is chosen as the family system's donor. Do not become somebody: be the family's food.

---

The longer you avoid making a decision so as to remain good in everybody's eyes, the greater the risk that you will end up bad in everybody's eyes.

---

Constantly looking for confrontation in 'Well, I disagree' mode is often about wanting to be noticed somehow rather than searching for the truth.

---

Euphoria is given to us to begin recovering after solving a difficult task. If it arrives before the task is solved, less strength remains for solving it.
