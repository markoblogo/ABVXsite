# **Market Fronts and Partner Landings in Focus**

Market fronts make a complex infrastructure project understandable to people who are not already inside its workflow. The pages and concepts in this group explain commercial services, partner relationships, risk tools and professional resources through different public formats. Their common job is to establish context and make the next conversation more specific.

A landing page should communicate who it is for, what it currently describes and what action is available. A deck, library or service page may support a different stage of evaluation.
