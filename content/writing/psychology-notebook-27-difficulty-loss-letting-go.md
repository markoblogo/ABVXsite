_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The greater the hope that an inner lack can be filled from outside, the greater the vulnerability.

---

Grief means experiencing and letting go of something that is impossible to let go of. For all its heaviness, the process later brings freedom.

---

And yet many people live as if there were a world bank of suffering, and if you suffer properly, that bank later pays out big dividends.

---

For many people, suffering provides a sense of biography, and giving it up is frightening: ‘Where am I in this scheme, then?’

---

Constant regret is like being unable to grieve: returning to a loss instead of letting it go.

---

It is always interesting to examine what makes suffering possible. What material and human resources sustain the possibility of remaining in the unbearable?

---

The range of plots for suffering is usually very narrow. Jouissance from the same old thing; suffering multi-jouissance is available to very few.

---

- If everything goes my way, I am bored. If it does not go my way, I am in pain.

---

A constant sense of depleted energy may be connected with a loss that has not been lived through.

---

Living through grief and letting go of a loss does not mean betraying what was lost.

---

Grieving is the gradual withdrawal of emotional investment from what will not be.

---

In the dramatic question ‘Are you hurting me accidentally or deliberately?’, the answer is usually not either-or but both-and.

---

Blaming is a trick for avoiding grief.

---

Pity is not the same as compassion. Compassion means empathising from an equal position. Pity is a way to place yourself above the person you pity.

---

Blame is a way of avoiding grief. Someone who has grieved does not look for someone to blame.

---

Suffering is a way of replacing inner movement with external bustle.

---

Indifference is a sign that something still hurts you. When it does not hurt, you feel steady rather than indifferent.

---

Giving up suffering is complicated by this: without suffering, you have to face the fact that the world is not evil and terrible, and that a person has no enemies but themselves.

---

Pity is a variety of contempt.

---

Endless regret is a way of avoiding grief.
