_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

Therapy is the transformation of pain into a story.

---

From supervision:

Love alone is not enough where professional help is needed.

---

Sometimes quitting therapy at the drop of a hat is the only way to say “no” to anything in life.

---

It is useful for a therapist to revisit the inner room containing cases in which something went wrong.

---

Rest is an important part of work. Therapy cannot replace the need, sometimes, to just fuck about.

---

Psychological resistance rests on the fact that the psyche cares less about what is better than about what is more familiar.

---

Dependency is sabotage and rebellion against everyday life, although any dependency eventually becomes more routine than all the routines of everyday life.

---

If you constantly feel bad and not okay, therapy is a way of discovering why being not okay feels so okay to you.

---

People often come to therapy asking to “put everything back the way it was”, although the way it was led to the way it is now.

---

What is psychological adaptation? It is a sense that the level of danger has decreased, so the organism no longer has to mobilise every time it meets a challenge.

---

Therapy is meeting your real self rather than an invented one. Sometimes people put enormous effort into sabotaging therapy to avoid that meeting.

---

People often turn to a therapist because of pain and/or boredom. Interestingly, boredom masks pain, while pain is a good distraction from boredom.

---

Psychological defences are the psyche’s response to a threatened or actual disruption of its picture of the world. Where a gap opens and adaptation is absent, a psychological defence appears.

---

Resilience is the ability to return to a balanced state after a threat or challenge has been overcome. It matters greatly to our sense of psychological well-being.

---

Sometimes the road to better mental health is blocked by the fear that, once you improve, people will expect you to function like a healthy person. You will no longer be able to excuse yourself with “but I’m just a helpless little creature, and my head’s gone cuckoo”.

---

A troubling psychological symptom does not mean the psyche has suddenly turned against you. It is how the psyche is trying to cope with unbearable circumstances, inside and/or outside.

---

Dependency usually takes far more energy than autonomy would. It is endless inflation in the psychological economy.

---

It is surprising when something frightening turns out to be an ally during therapy. Yet it makes sense: the more difficult the situation, the more fearsome the allies needed within it.

---

Sometimes more hope is placed in a therapist than in Father Christmas.

“Can’t you change the world during our work so that it suits me better?”

---

Sometimes psychological defences are so poorly adapted that it is like walking through a metropolis in plate armour and answering questions with:

“I feel safer this way.”
