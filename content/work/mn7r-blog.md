MN7R Blog is the operational media layer around the MN7R brokerage workflow ecosystem.

The blog publishes field notes and research on commodity brokerage operations, execution discipline, role-based workflow design, team visibility and market-infrastructure practice.

Rather than functioning as a generic writing archive, the resource supports the MN7R operating layer:
- explaining brokerage workflow problems,
- documenting execution and communication patterns,
- connecting market operations to structured software surfaces,
- and giving the broader Focus ecosystem a live editorial signal around brokerage infrastructure.

The current Focus card and homepage entry are driven by the live MN7R RSS feed. When a new article is published, the visible title, excerpt, image and destination link update automatically from the latest feed item.
