# Market Fronts & Landing Pages for Commodity Infrastructure

Commodity-market infrastructure can be difficult to explain from a product name or repository alone. Traders, partners and other market participants need to understand what a system is for, which operating problem it addresses and what they can inspect or do next. A market front provides that public-facing layer.

In this catalogue, market fronts include product sites, partner landings, commercial frameworks and explanatory materials connected to agro-commodity systems. [AMI Books: Physical Commodity Markets](/work/ami-books-physical-commodity-markets) is a professional library for brokers, traders, farmers and market operators. [Liqua](/work/liqua) describes a liquidity and execution layer for physical commodity markets. [Trade Solution EU](/work/trade-solution-eu) presents a commercial and logistics framework for market execution and supply-chain coordination. The [Cropto Market Risk Tools Deck](/work/cropto-market-risk-deck) explains a market-risk concept linked to local spot-index infrastructure.

## A landing page has a different job from the platform

A landing page introduces a product or service; it does not prove every operational claim or replace the underlying software. The best public explanation gives readers enough structure to judge relevance: the intended user, the problem, the key terms, the current form of the offer and any visible next step.

This distinction is especially useful when the project is early-stage or spans several participants. A site can make assumptions and boundaries inspectable while linking to a product, repository, methodology or contact point. It can also connect an offer to the market infrastructure that supports it.

## Connecting the market and the offer

Clear market fronts reduce the work required to understand a commodity product. They make market language more consistent, provide a stable reference for partners and help different audiences find the right entry point. They should stay specific to the project and its current evidence.
