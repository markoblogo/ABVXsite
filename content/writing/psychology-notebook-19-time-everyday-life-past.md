_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

A realistic assessment of your limits gives you real freedom.

---

A healed wound frees up a great deal of strength for living.

---

Few things make us as unhappy as being fixated on happiness.

---

What is missing inside cannot be satisfied from outside, only anaesthetised for a while.

---

Anxiety means trying to prevent everything in the future while paying for it with the present.

---

Of all orientations, the main one is orientation in time, space and your own identity.

---

A reminder: a crisis is the most favourable time for personal development.

---

Remaining voluntarily isolated for a long time is a choice in favour of death out of fear of life.

---

Being fixated on finding arguments for your unhappiness prevents you from seeing opportunities to change your life.

---

Suffering gives us a sense of existing because it knocks out the other settings for the sensations life can offer.

---

The past is a suitcase: the more unnecessary things you pack into it, the harder it is to drag into the future.

---

The more our attention is occupied by unresolved questions from the past, the fewer resources remain for responding in the present.

---

I would like everybody to stop giving a damn about their problems and devote their whole lives to solving mine.

---

As a person lives, so is their life.

The shape of our everyday existence eventually becomes our fate.

---

Investing in something dead is a dead investment. Putting energy into something that is not viable will not make it viable.

---

Paradoxically, greed sometimes slows down grieving. We cannot bear to let go of the past, even a past that hurts like hell.

---

Our perception assembles reality from an endless flow of possibilities. The unconscious and our inner attitudes are the mould through which we create our lives.

---

Rejecting your past instead of integrating past experience into the present deprives you of support. Autobiographical memory does not like too many empty spaces.

---

Sometimes, while bargaining with the fact of inevitable death, a person gives up the joy of living. What is the point of rejoicing or enjoying anything if I am going to die anyway?

---

Often there is no harder road than looking for the quickest and easiest option. Hoping to get something for nothing means building your idea of life on survivorship bias.
