ABVX OS coordinates personal AI work without collapsing professional roles into one generic assistant. It routes a task to the appropriate role, keeps evidence and portfolio state local, and preserves explicit review gates for consequential actions.

Its current companion surfaces are CoqPi for reviewed multilingual conversations, Pocket OS for portable intake, and Book Landings for approved publishing outputs.
