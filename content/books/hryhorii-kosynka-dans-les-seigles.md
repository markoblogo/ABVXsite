Hryhorii Kosynka: Dans les seigles et autres nouvelles is a free French edition from the Modernisme Ukrainien publishing line.

The book presents Kosynka’s short fiction as part of the broader effort to make early twentieth-century Ukrainian modernist literature readable in French. His prose is compact, tense and rooted in the social and psychological fractures of post-revolutionary Ukraine.

Inside the Modernisme Ukrainien ecosystem, this edition works as both a literary translation and a cultural recovery project. It helps place Ukrainian prose of the 1920s alongside the wider European modernist map, where Ukrainian authors are still too often missing from the canonical frame.

The edition is designed for:

- French-speaking readers discovering Ukrainian modernism,
- readers of short fiction and modernist prose,
- students and researchers looking for accessible Ukrainian literary texts,
- and anyone following the ABVX translation and publishing work around the Ukrainian “Executed Renaissance”.

The free PDF format keeps the work easy to access while the series site provides the broader publishing context, related authors and companion material.
