Pictiq: A Visual Protocol for Short Messages is a free illustrated field guide to a compact visual protocol for everyday cross-language communication. It introduces the Core lexicon, simple composable rules, context packs and formats designed for real surfaces: signs, cards, luggage tags, phone lock screens and more.

The handbook is for readers who want to understand how a small icon vocabulary can support pointing, questions, urgency, negation and short portable messages without attempting to replace natural language or long-form writing.

It also records early experiments in visual storytelling, poetry, computer vision and language models. Download the PDF or EPUB, or visit the Pictiq site for the live protocol and project context.
