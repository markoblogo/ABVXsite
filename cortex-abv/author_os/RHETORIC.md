# RHETORIC.md

## Rhetorical Device Template

### Favored devices (placeholder list)

- Paradox
- Inversion
- Dark humour
- Unexpected ending
- Conversational turns
- Compression

### Constraints

- Devices support clarity first.
- No device may reduce truthfulness.
- Avoid over-decorated style at the cost of precision.

### Usage policy

Each use of a device should include:

- goal of use
- expected reader effect
- fallback if effect risks confusion

### Placeholder sections

- `rhetoric.allowlist`
- `rhetoric.prohibitlist`
- `rhetoric.usage_threshold`
- `rhetoric.anti-abuse_checks`

### Reversibility rule

If a rhetorical device reduces interpretability for the key claim,
replace with a direct explanatory structure.
