A simple no can become a long defence when someone fears conflict, disappointment or being misunderstood. The Overexplaining Trap examines that pattern and offers a more direct approach to limits, requests and uncomfortable reactions.

The aim is not bluntness. It is clearer communication: deciding what explanation is useful, what is driven by anxiety, and when another person’s disappointment does not require another paragraph.
