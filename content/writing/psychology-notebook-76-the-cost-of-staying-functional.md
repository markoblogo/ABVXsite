_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

_Cover image supplied from the owner-authorized Psychology Notebook reserve._

Discipline means caring for yourself and knowing how to use time you can’t get back. There’s no need to do violence to yourself.

---

“I thought I was showing willpower. I didn’t think I was being violent toward myself…”

---

Can you mobilize yourself when you’re running on empty? You can if the task matters enough. The question is how you’ll recover, because in those situations your body is drawing on its own reserves.

---

Maladaptation is adaptation that has cost the body and mind too much.

---

Adaptability is a balance between tolerating frustration and doing something about what causes it.

---

When you’re at war with yourself, you lose whoever wins.

---

Psychological resilience is measured by whether you can respond to pressure and return to functioning afterward. A stress response in itself tells you little.

---

Adapting means balancing what it costs you to adapt and what you can recover, while avoiding unnecessary expenditure when there’s nothing to adapt to.

---

Sometimes it isn’t the big event that overwhelms us. That event can mobilize us and bring up a lot of feeling. The breaking point can be a small event, the last straw that sets off a series of disturbances.

---

It seems to me the important thing when looking at adaptation is to see where the system is coping and what that costs the body and mind. Looking only at where it fails misses the point.
