# AI Override Template

## Scope

This override applies to AI systems, agent behavior, and technical automation text.

## Extends

- `PROMPT_INTEGRATION`
- `ANTI_PATTERNS`
- `VALUES`

## Replaces

PLACEHOLDER: `NONE` (default)

## Rule additions

- `autonomy_level`: placeholders for what AI can propose vs execute
- `decision_trace`: mandatory fields for claim and override traceability
- `safety`: placeholders for constrained action and verification gates

## Governance

- All AI-facing claims must map to evidence or declared uncertainty.
- No blind action paths without explicit owner approval in write-capable contexts.
