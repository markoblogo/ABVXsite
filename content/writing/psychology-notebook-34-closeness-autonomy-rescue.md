_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

A partner is a symptom brought outside.

---

A sign of counterdependency: getting hooked on rejecting partners.

---

Self-sufficiency is not isolation. It is the ability both to be with others and to spend time alone without looking for something to distract or entertain you.

---

Interestingly, an avoidant attachment style often goes together with multiple dependencies.

---

Whatever you run from, and however far you run, you still take your internal conflict with you.

---

Envy is hatred of someone who possesses what you love.

---

Codependency is not a two-person relationship. It is always a threesome: two people and a dependency.

---

In a codependent relationship, the codependent person uses the dependent person to avoid encountering their own pain.

---

A family prohibition on growing up often comes together with a prohibition on sexuality.

---

An unsuitable partner, with whom you feel bad but the relationship continues and continues, is a way of punishing yourself.

---

Resilience to stress is determined by the balance between curiosity about the new and fear of change. More resilient people are more curious.

---

Treating others as objects usually goes together with treating yourself as an object and a function.

---

The “bad” person is often someone we use to service our own goodness.

---

Separation means recognising a loved one’s right to experience difficult feelings without plunging into them yourself.

---

A person’s enemies tell us about them as well as their friends do. Tell me who your enemy is, and I will tell you what is in your shadow.

---

If a person cannot receive love, no amount of it will relieve the unbearable hunger.

---

An anxious-avoidant attachment style is a recursive practice: running from intimacy in terror at the fact that you are running from intimacy.

---

With a counterdependent pattern, an imagined ideal helps justify avoiding intimacy. If someone is not perfect, there is no point even starting.

---

Nothing distracts us from our own problems quite like trying to rescue someone.

---

Hurrying is one expression of the “flight” response. Yet in the end, hurrying is a reliable way to slow down and complicate any task.
