_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

A person always knows best what is best for them and acts according to their own idea of what is good.

---

The dopamine-driven craving for novelty: better worse, as long as it's different.

---

People who fixate on experiences can even turn self-denial into an experience. A deficit becomes a surplus too. For them, developing moderation matters more than strict restrictions.

---

Being is presence and the unfolding of events.

---

Drama is a concentration on the impossibility of the unimportant, distracting us from the possibilities of the important.

---

The intensity of passions usually shows one thing: a surplus of free time.

---

Imagination can be a cozy refuge until it becomes a dangerous trap from which one cannot return to reality.

---

The absence of joy as a survival strategy: if you are joyful, you are vulnerable, and we can't have that.

---

‘It is important to be a safe place for yourself.’

I think therapy is about learning how to become such a place for yourself.

---

The upside of fantasy: you can escape reality into dreams.

The downside: reality and its troubles do not disappear while you are fantasizing.
