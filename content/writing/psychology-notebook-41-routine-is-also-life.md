_A thematic selection of translated notebook fragments._

The difficulty with enduring everyday routine lies in the hope that real life is somewhere out there, beyond the routine. But without routine, life falls apart and stops feeling like life. Besides, everyday routine is life too.

---

An organised everyday life is the foundation of great things.

---

Many people try to hack the fact that the way you live determines the life you have. I want to live any old way, but have a life that is just right.

---

Extraordinary effort is an important part of achievement. But if there are no achievements and life is nothing but extraordinary effort, that is maladaptation.

---

Discipline is freedom, not a prison. You cannot be free when you cannot even manage your own time.

---

Discipline works where motivation does not.
