MN7R Agro Commodity Brokerage is a practical introduction to operational brokerage workflows inside physical agro-commodity markets.

The project focuses on:
- execution discipline,
- brokerage coordination,
- operational workflows,
- market structure,
- trading-system organization,
- and infrastructure logic around commodity operations.

Rather than treating brokerage as abstract finance, the book approaches it as:
- operational coordination,
- structured execution,
- information management,
- workflow continuity,
- and market infrastructure.

The free Ukrainian edition acts as the first publishing surface inside the broader MN7R Commodity Brokerage Library ecosystem.

Future planned extensions include:
- English editions,
- Amazon editions,
- expanded workflow manuals,
- infrastructure-focused brokerage publications,
- and related market-system texts.
